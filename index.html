What is a Percentage Calculator?

A percentage calculator helps you solve common problems involving percentages. It can find a percentage of a number, determine what percentage one value is of another, and calculate percentage increases or decreases.

How to use this calculator

  1. Select the type of percentage problem.
  2. Enter the required values.
  3. Press Calculate Percentage.
  4. Read the result and formula shown below the calculator.

Common uses

Percentages are used for school marks, discounts, test scores, statistics, price changes, profit and loss, interest and everyday comparisons.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number.
How do I find what percentage X is of Y?Use (X ÷ Y) × 100.
How is percentage increase calculated?Use ((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100.
Can percentages be greater than 100%?Yes. For example, 150% means one and a half times the reference amount.
